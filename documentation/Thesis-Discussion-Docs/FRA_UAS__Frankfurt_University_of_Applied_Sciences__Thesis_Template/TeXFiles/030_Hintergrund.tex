\chapter{Biological Foundation}
\label{chap:bio}

This chapter establishes the biological circuit that the model developed in this thesis.
The neocortical microcircuit and the cortico-thalamo-cortical loop in which it is embedded. The account is canonical rather than exhaustive.
It describes how the organisation that recurs accross cortical areas rather than the full quantitative detail of any one and it is drawn 
principally from the sensory neocortex, where the underlying anatomy and physiology are most fully characterised.
It rests on a small set of established sources:
\textcite{white1989} and the \emph{Handbook of Brain Microcircuits} \parencite{shepherd2018} for cortical structure, \textcite{douglas1989,douglas2004} for the canonical microcircuit, \textcite{rudy2011} for the inhibitory cell classes, and \textcite{sherman1998,sherman2013} for the thalamus and its cortical connections.
